Wealth

Stocks, ETFs and mutual funds come from the holdings files you export from Groww (Import button) or from what you type in. Your SIP, step-up and return assumptions stay when you import again. EPF, crypto, loans, credit and insurance are entered by hand.

Projection: each holding grows monthly at its return, the SIP is added every month, steps up once a year and stops after its SIP years. Returns are assumptions you set, not forecasts. This is not investment advice.
